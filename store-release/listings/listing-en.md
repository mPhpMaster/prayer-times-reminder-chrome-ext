## Full description

Prayer Times Reminder helps you never miss a prayer — and gently steps in when it's time, so you can put the screen down and pray.

**Accurate prayer times, offline**
Fajr, Sunrise, Duha, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha, calculated on your device with the trusted adhan engine — no account, no internet required after setup. Choose your city and calculation method (Umm al-Qura, ISNA, MWL, Egypt, and more).

**Prayer Times Break — a focus lock at prayer time**
When a prayer arrives, the app can gently lock the screen for a few minutes so you step away and pray, then unlocks automatically. Optional, fully under your control:
• Set the lock duration
• Silent (Do Not Disturb) during prayer — mutes calls and notifications
• Optional manual unlock
• Never interrupts an active phone call — it waits until your call ends

**Smart reminders**
A clear countdown to the next prayer, plus notifications so a prayer never slips by.

**Dhikr through your day**
Gentle, periodic dhikr reminders (from Hisn al-Muslim and more) — as a floating card that appears and fades on its own.

**Made for everyone**
• 8 languages: English, العربية, اردو, Français, Español, हिन्दी, Bahasa Indonesia, Deutsch
• Arabic-Indic or Western numerals
• Two themes: Midnight Emerald and Classic
• Hijri and Gregorian dates

**Private by design**
Your location stays on your device and is only used to compute prayer times. No tracking, no ads.

Available on Chrome, Windows, and Android.
