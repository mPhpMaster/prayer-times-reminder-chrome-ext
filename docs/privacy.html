Privacy Policy — Prayer Times Reminder

Last updated: 27 September 2026 · Applies to the Chrome extension, the Windows app, and the Android app.

Prayer Times Reminder (“the app”) is built to respect your privacy. It shows no ads and includes no third‑party analytics or tracking. The only data the developer receives is what you choose to share through the optional game account in the Android app or the Chrome extension (see “Dhikr game” below). Prayer times, prayer alerts and every prayer setting work without any account.

What the app stores

The app stores your preferences locally on your device only:

  • Your chosen location — a country + city, or (if you opt in) your approximate coordinates from the device’s location prompt.
  • Your language, theme, date format, and number style.
  • Prayer‑lock settings (enabled or not, duration, silent/Do‑Not‑Disturb, manual unlock).
  • Dhikr settings (enabled or not, interval, on‑screen position).
  • A small cache of today’s prayer times and your country’s city list, so the app opens quickly.
  • If you play the optional dhikr game: your progress (tasks finished, points, times), game settings, the recognized text of a task you have not finished yet (so you can resume it; it is removed when the task is done), and, if you sign in, your username, email address and the access token for this device.

This data never leaves your device except as described below.

Location

Location is used only to calculate prayer times. You can simply pick a city by name; using your device’s location is optional. When used, your coordinates are processed to compute prayer times and are not stored remotely or shared for advertising or tracking.

Data sent to third parties

To calculate times and fill the city list, the app may contact these free public services, sending only the minimum needed — never any account or personal identifier:

  • AlAdhan API (api.aladhan.com) — your city/country and the calculation method, to return prayer times. This is only a fallback for a saved city whose coordinates could not be found; normally all apps compute prayer times offline, on your device.
  • CountriesNow API (countriesnow.space) — a country name, to return its list of cities.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) — the name of the city you select (with its country), to find its coordinates for the offline calculation and, when the interface language is Arabic, to show its Arabic label. Your device coordinates are not sent.

These services receive your request the way any website does (including your IP address) and handle it under their own privacy policies. The developer receives none of this data.

Permissions

The prayer lock covers the screen (or the browser tab, in the extension) during prayer and can silence notifications. These features are optional, are turned on by you, and can be disabled at any time in the app’s settings.

Chrome extension

The extension’s popup is the prayer-times page and never needs an account. Its permissions:

  • alarms, notifications — schedule and show prayer-time alerts (and, if you turn them on in the game, “tasks are open” reminders).
  • storage — keep your settings, cached times and game progress on your device.
  • geolocation — optional; only when you choose “Use my location”.
  • scripting and the optional access to your open tabs (<all_urls>) — place the prayer-break overlay and the dhikr card on your open tabs. Tab access is not granted at install; the extension asks for it when you first turn on the prayer break or dhikr reminders, and you can decline. The overlay and card do not read page content, forms, passwords or browsing history, and send nothing from the page anywhere.
  • offscreen — play the adhan or chime packaged with the extension at prayer time.
  • identity — only for the optional “Continue with Google” in the game (see below).

The microphone is not an extension permission: the game page asks Chrome for it the first time you tap “Start reading”, and you can revoke it in Chrome’s site settings.

Android app

On Android, settings and cached prayer times are stored in the app’s private storage on your phone. If you use your device’s location, the coordinates are used on the phone to calculate prayer times and are never sent anywhere. Only the requests listed above (a country or city name) and, if you create one, your game account data (see “Dhikr game”) leave the device, over HTTPS.

The app asks for these Android permissions. Each one is used only for the purpose shown:

  • Location (approximate/precise) — optional; to calculate prayer times for where you are, on the device.
  • Notifications — prayer-time reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) — so reminders and the prayer lock start at the exact minute of each prayer.
  • Full-screen alerts and display over other apps — to show the prayer lock at prayer time, and the small dhikr card that disappears by itself.
  • Foreground service — keeps the prayer lock running for the duration you chose, with a visible notification.
  • Do Not Disturb access — optional “silent during prayer”; your previous setting is restored when the lock ends.
  • Ignore battery optimizations — so the phone’s battery saver does not stop reminders from arriving.
  • Run at startup — to re-schedule reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Microphone — only while you tap the microphone in the game to recite a dhikr (see below).
  • Internet — for the city lookups listed above and, if you create a game account, the game server.

The app never ends or blocks a phone call: if a prayer lock is due during a call, it waits until the call ends.

Dhikr game (Android app and Chrome extension)

The Android app and Chrome extension open on prayer times. The dhikr game is an optional page opened from the prayer-times interface. Playing works locally without an account, and your progress is saved on your device. Two parts are optional:

  • Game account. If you create one, you sign in with your email address and a password, or with your Google account, and choose a public username shown on the leaderboard (never your email). Our game server (prayer-times.sarhsoft.com, over HTTPS) then stores: your email address; your password only as a one-way bcrypt hash (never the password itself); if you use Google, Google’s account ID for you and the email Google confirms (we do not store your Google name, photo, or any other Google data, and never see your Google password); your username and optional display name; the dhikr tasks you finish (task, points, and the time you started and finished); the players you follow; whether you hid your progress; and, for each signed-in device, a hash of its access token (never the token itself) and when it was last used. The email is used only to sign you in and, if you ask, to send a password-reset code, which is valid for 15 minutes and stored only as a hash. Your username, display name and monthly points are shown to other signed-in players on the leaderboard and your profile unless you hide your progress. The server does not store your IP address in your account. Your location, city and prayer times are never sent — prayer windows are computed on your device. You can hide your progress from others at any time. In the Chrome extension, “Continue with Google” opens Chrome’s own Google sign-in window (the extension’s identity permission); the extension receives only the sign-in token that Google issues for the game, and passes it to the game server to confirm your account.
  • Voice. When you tap the microphone to recite, the app uses the platform’s speech recognition — Android’s (Google’s voice input) in the Android app, Chrome’s built-in speech recognition in the extension, which asks for microphone permission the first time. Your voice is processed by that service under Google’s privacy policy. The app only receives the recognized text and compares it with the dhikr on your device. The app does not record or keep your voice. The recognized text is never sent to us; it stays on your device only until the task it belongs to is finished.

Deleting your data

Game account: in the app, open حسابي (My account) › احذف حسابي (Delete my account). This permanently deletes your email, password hash, Google link, username, points, progress and follows from our server at once. If you no longer have the app, see how to request deletion.

On your device: to delete everything the app keeps on your device, clear the app’s data (Android: Settings › Apps › Prayer Times Reminder › Storage › Clear data) or uninstall it (Chrome: remove the extension).

Children

The app does not ask for age. The optional game account uses an email address (or Google sign-in) and a public username. A parent or guardian should supervise account creation where required by applicable law.
