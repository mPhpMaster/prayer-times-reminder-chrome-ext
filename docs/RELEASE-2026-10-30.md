## What changed

- **Android:** Do-Not-Disturb is lifted after the prayer lock even when the
  process died mid-lock. The pre-lock interruption filter is persisted (it was
  a process static, so a re-launched lock captured the app's own ALARMS filter
  as the user's and "restored" DND back on); a one-shot restore alarm fires at
  `unlockAt + 5 s`; stale locks are swept on app resume, boot, the next
  scheduled fire and sticky-service restart.
- **Game names:** letters, digits, `_ . -` and single spaces, 3–20 characters,
  at least one letter or digit (`Moh. Al-Safadi` is valid; `...` is not).
- **Admin page:** an admin cannot remove their own admin row.
- **Extension:** `activeLockReady` is declared before the listener that awaits
  it; a `gameState` change on the new-tab page loads `game-tasks.js` before
  rendering the tasks card.
