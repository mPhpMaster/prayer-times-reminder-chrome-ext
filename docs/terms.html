Prayer times

Prayer times are calculated locally from the location and calculation method you select. Results may differ from a mosque or local authority. Check the method and location before relying on reminders. The app is an aid and does not replace an authoritative local timetable.

Optional game and account

Prayer times, settings, and reminders do not require an account. The optional dhikr game can be played locally. An account is needed only for server features such as leaderboards and following. You may register with email and password or Google and choose a public username. Your username, display name and monthly points are visible to other signed-in players unless you hide your progress.

You are responsible for your account access and public username. Do not impersonate others, disrupt the service, automate abuse, or submit unlawful content. Accounts used to abuse the service may be restricted.

Game points and rankings are participation features with no monetary value. They may change when scoring rules or verified tasks change.

Voice recognition and third-party services

Voice missions use the speech-recognition service provided by the platform (in Chrome, Google’s speech service through the browser; on Android, the phone’s speech recognition). Recognition may be inaccurate, so a correct reading is occasionally not counted. Location-name lookup, city lists, Google sign-in, and fallback prayer-time requests depend on the third-party services identified in the Privacy Policy and are also subject to their terms.

Account deletion

You may permanently delete a signed-in game account from My account. This removes the server account, points, progress, follows, and sign-in links. Local game progress may remain on the device until app data is cleared or the app or extension is removed. See Delete account.
